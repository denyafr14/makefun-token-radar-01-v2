Token Launch Radar

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × years tokenless. The multiple is the median market-cap/TVL of the protocol’s own category among protocols whose token already trades, clamped to 0.10–10×; growth is its 7-day TVL change, the age factor rewards years run with no token. Protocols that already have a token are the yardstick, never board rows. No name is special-cased: one formula scores every row.
